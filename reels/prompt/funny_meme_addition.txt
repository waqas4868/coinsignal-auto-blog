😂 FUNNY / MEME STYLE RULES (CRITICAL)

The Reel must NOT feel like a serious documentary, formal news report, lecture, or boring financial presentation.

The storytelling must be FUNNY, MEME-STYLE, RELATABLE, and ENTERTAINING while still explaining the crypto news accurately.

Use:
• Funny stickman reactions
• Exaggerated facial expressions
• Relatable crypto situations
• Meme-style visual jokes
• Comedic misunderstandings
• Unexpected reactions
• Funny props
• Light sarcasm
• Internet-style humor
• Crypto trader/investor relatable moments

Examples of visual humor:
• Stickman seeing a huge Bitcoin number and almost falling from his chair
• Stickman checking his portfolio and instantly regretting it
• Character confidently explaining something, then getting shocked by the next chart
• One character reacting dramatically while another stays calm
• Funny use of phones, laptops, charts, calculators, coffee, wallets, rockets, red/green candles, etc.

IMPORTANT:
The humor must support the NEWS STORY rather than replace it.

Do NOT turn the Reel into random comedy.

The actual crypto news, numbers, events, and meaning must remain clear and factually grounded.

Every scene should try to contain at least ONE engaging visual or reaction that makes the viewer smile, laugh, or become curious.

The overall feeling should be:

CRYPTO NEWS + EDUCATION + MEMES + COMEDY + STORYTELLING

NOT:

SERIOUS NEWS REPORT + LECTURE

The tone should feel like a funny crypto creator explaining today's biggest news to a friend.
